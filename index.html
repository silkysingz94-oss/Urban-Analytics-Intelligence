08 / METHODOLOGY

Street network indicators are derived from OpenStreetMap ways. Network nodes are estimated by snapping road endpoints within approximately 15 metres. This is a simplified connectivity measure, not a complete graph-theoretic centrality analysis. Road density is calculated as total mapped road length divided by the selected polygon area. Buildings and POIs represent mapped OSM features only.
